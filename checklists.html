Free checklists

Check your company in 15 minutes.

Two plain-English checklists from our engineers: one for how your teams use AI, one for the apps they build with it. Ask for the full version of either and we’ll send a fix for every item.

Secrets and AI AI-built apps

Checklist 01 · For every team

10 places your secrets are leaking into AI.

Check your company in 15 minutes. The full version includes a plain-English fix for each one.

  1. Work done in chatbots on personal or free accounts
  2. Client documents pasted in to clean up or summarize
  3. Passwords, API keys, and database logins in code shared with AI coding tools
  4. AI note-takers joining client calls
  5. Browser extensions that read every page you open
  6. AI features switched on inside software you already pay for
  7. Shared chat links that anyone with the link can open
  8. Agents with access to email, files, or payments and no approval step
  9. Products built on public AI with customer data in every request
  10. No written AI policy, so nobody knows what’s allowed

Checklist 02 · For builders

Before you ship an AI-built app: 10 checks.

For apps your team vibe coded or built with AI coding assistants. Run through it before customers or coworkers rely on the app. The full version includes a plain-English fix for each one.

  1. Passwords, API keys, and database logins written into the code
  2. No login, or one shared login, on an app with company data
  3. Packages the AI suggested that nobody checked, or that don’t exist
  4. Customer data sent to an AI provider with every request
  5. No record of who used the app or what it did
  6. Error messages that show your data or your settings
  7. Inputs that let a user give the AI new instructions
  8. An app only one person understands, with no named owner
  9. No plan for updates when a model or a package changes
  10. No one has reviewed the code before people rely on it
