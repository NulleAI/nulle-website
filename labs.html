DREA

Your research. Your data. Your terms.

A private AI desktop app for searching and researching your own documents. Everything runs on your Mac, tuned for Apple Silicon. Built with Tauri, Rust, and Python.

Works with the internet off

All AI runs on your Mac. Your documents never leave it.

Search by meaning

Find answers across PDFs, images, and more, even without the exact words.

Built for Apple Silicon

Fast on M-series chips, with no cloud required.

Safe access to outside AI

Use Claude, ChatGPT, and others through a trust proxy that removes sensitive details before anything leaves.

CreatorCam

Shoot once, post everywhere.

A dual-camera recording app for iPhone that captures full-resolution portrait (9:16) and landscape (16:9) video at the same time. One tap, two synced files, saved straight to your Photos library.

Two cameras at once

Portrait and landscape from your wide and ultra-wide lenses, both full resolution, not crops.

Single-camera mode

Switch to one lens, with full front-camera support for talking-head videos and interviews.

Professional quality

4K and 1080p at 24, 30, or 60 fps. Optical zoom, MOV and MP4, GPU-accelerated processing.

Built for creators

Floating preview, countdown timer, and torch control, with files saved straight to Photos.
